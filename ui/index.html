Setup guide

  1. Power up

    Plug in both USB cables. Join the WebScope Wi-Fi, then open http://192.168.4.1.

  2. Build the button circuit

    Copy the picture: 3.3 V → button → 10 kΩ → 10 kΩ → ground. The middle of the two resistors is what you'll measure.

  3. Connect the leads

    Black GND lead to the − rail first. Then the yellow CH1 lead to the middle of the resistors.

  4. Waiting for signal…

    Go Live and press the button

    Tap Live, then press the button a few times. The line jumps up while it's held.

    You should seeReleased ≈ 0 VPressed ≈ 1.65 V
Input limit: 0 – 2.5 V Anything higher can damage the FPGA. Never connect wall power.
